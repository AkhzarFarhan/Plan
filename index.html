⚠️ 3 Golden Driver Rules

1. Single Driver Safety

Front passenger stays awake at all times. Compulsory 10-min tea break every 2 hours.

2. Festival Crowd Bypass

Monday is Ganesh Chaturthi. Stick to beaches, falls, and peaks; skip crowded temple centers.

3. Ghat & Sea Caution

Use 2nd/3rd gear down Agumbe. No deep-sea swimming due to post-monsoon undertows.
